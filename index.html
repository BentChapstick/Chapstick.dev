Ufactory
7DOF Arm

A new project started upon the conclude of AutoDrive II. Conducted as part of the Robotic Systems Enterprise at Michigan Tech University to simulate, path-plan, and control a 7 Degree of Freedom Ufactory x-arm using ROS 2, Gazebo, Moveit, and Computer Vision through a mounted camera. Will be controlled in tandem with a second, smaller, Niryo 6 Degree of Freedom robotic arm, to perform congruent tasks with both arms.
